## Objections (from SALES_PLAYBOOK §4, adjusted to October rules)

| Objection | Reply direction |
|---|---|
| "No track record" | Don't invent one. "We're new, so we let you check everything: documents with your advocate, dated site progress, and a founder you can call." |
| "Why no rate?" | "Terms depend on how you'll use the space. A ten-minute call lets us give you a real answer instead of a teaser." |
| "RERA not registered" (Salavakkam) | "Correct. DTCP approval and RERA registration are to follow, and we aren't taking bookings until they're in place." |
| "B+G+3 is small" | "It's deliberately a single building on a corner plot: one occupier or a few, with two road frontages." |
| "Completion is far off" | "Target Q1 2028. You can follow progress on our dated site updates, and pre-lease terms let you plan around it." |
| "Basaveshwara Nagar isn't prime" | Facts only: metro distances, frontages, locality data with sources (Sunday Standard). No value predictions. |
| "Plots far from Chennai" | Location facts with sources; no appreciation claims. |
| "I'll wait" | Respect it. Offer to keep them posted (opt-in) and invite a site visit when convenient. |

## Never say (any channel)

Rates, rents, totals, payment schedules; RERA or DTCP numbers; returns, yields, ROI, "doubling";
any possession date for Salavakkam; lender tie-ups; architect names; revenue or headcount;
testimonials, ratings, buyer lists; projects in other cities; anything about other developers;
financial, tax or legal advice; "floors available" or any scarcity.

## Chatbot lines not to reuse

"We don't pursue awards"; "relationship with several leading financial institutions";
"generally meet eligibility of major lenders"; "founder leads design"; "approvals secured".
