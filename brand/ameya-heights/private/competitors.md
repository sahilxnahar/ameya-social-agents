# Ameya Heights: competitive context (INTERNAL, never named in public copy)

From the Competitive Analysis (16 Jul), the Competitive & SEO Deep-Dive (13 Aug) and the owner's
benchmark set (21 Jul). The claims linter blocks these names in drafts.

## Chennai plotted and boutique

- G Square Housing: scale benchmark, very large customer base, hundreds of locality pages, video testimonials.
- HiLiving: closest peer; testimonials and scarcity cues (which we don't copy).
- Royal Land & Developers, VGN, Casagrand, Radiance, TVS Emerald, Alliance.

## Bengaluru commercial and large developers

- Sobha, Prestige, Brigade, Godrej, Puravankara (closest model to grow into), Emaar India.
- Owner's benchmark set: DLF, Godrej, Lodha, Oberoi.
- bangaloreoffice.com wins Basaveshwara Nagar micro-market searches with a Google Business Profile
  and long local content.
- Portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing, NoBroker) own generic searches; don't chase those terms.

## Name collisions in search

Ameya Heights Kalyan East, Ameya Eastern Heights, KG Ameya, Ameya Group. Goal: own page one for
"Ameya Heights" with our profiles, listings and press.

## How we position (without naming anyone)

- Approvals and documents before anyone pays; a named founder who answers.
- Dated, unedited progress photos; no fake renders, no manufactured proof.
- Micro-market topics we can win: Basaveshwara Nagar commercial, West Bengaluru occupiers,
  Salavakkam and Kanchipuram district plots, NRI how-to content.
- Avoid what they do: heavy scarcity, "dream home" copy, testimonial walls we don't have.
