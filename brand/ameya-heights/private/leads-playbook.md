# Ameya Heights: leads playbook (INTERNAL)

From the CRM (ameyacrm v16.17 schema and starter automations), the Apps Script map, Roadmap-1 and
the action plans (Jul–Aug 2026).

## Where leads come from today

- Website form → Apps Script → Google Sheet, with an owner email and an acknowledgement from hi@.
  (Planned: move leads into the CRM; the Sheet is a data-protection risk.)
- WhatsApp click-to-chat on +91 99629 65000, answered by a person. The CRM WhatsApp webhook is off by
  the owner's choice.
- Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn DMs and comments; Google reviews and Q&A.
- Portals: none live yet. A listing pack is ready.

## Stages (match the CRM)

NEW → CONTACTED → QUALIFIED → SITE_VISIT → NEGOTIATION → BOOKED → WON / LOST.
Temperature: HOT, WARM, COLD. Sources: WEBSITE, REFERRAL, WALK_IN, CAMPAIGN, PORTAL, NRI_DESK,
BROKER, OTHER. Record consent for MARKETING, WHATSAPP, CALLS separately.

Agents only draft replies and log to `reports/leads.md`. Stage changes happen in the CRM, by a person.

## Response targets

| Lead | Target |
|---|---|
| Any new enquiry | Draft reply within 1 hour; call task within 1 day |
| Website or portal lead | Same day, marked URGENT (portal leads go to several builders at once) |
| NRI | Flag to the owner the same day; offer a video walk-through |
| Large requirement (whole building, ₹1 Cr+ budget mentioned) | Flag to the owner for a call within the hour |
| Channel partner | Same-day acknowledgement |
| Site visit booked | Prep note the day before: what they asked, what to show |

## Site visits

- Primary call to action: book a site visit. Offer two concrete slots, or the booking link if the
  owner has shared one in `reports/questions.md`.
- From abroad: a video walk-through.

## Follow-up (opt-in only, owner-approved)

A five-touch sequence over about three weeks, WhatsApp or email depending on consent:
1. Thank you + document pack or brochure.
2. Latest dated progress photo.
3. One locality fact with a source.
4. Site-visit or video-call invitation with two slots.
5. A short check-in: "Is this still useful to you?" Stop if no reply.

Never more than one unprompted message a week. Stop immediately on "stop" or no interest.

## Every reply

- Answers the actual question from `facts.md` and `faq.md`.
- One next step.
- No rates, no payment details, no scarcity.
- WhatsApp free-form replies only within 24 hours of the person's last message.
