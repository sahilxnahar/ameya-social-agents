# Ameya Heights: audiences

Primary goal for every audience: **book a site visit**. Secondary: WhatsApp conversation, brochure request.

| Audience | What they want | Project | Best channels | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Businesses leasing space (clinics, offices, retail, showrooms) | Location, frontage, floor sizes, parking, timeline | FOUR·94 | Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Instagram | Never imply a sale; areas are "about" |
| Chennai plot buyers | Clear titles, approvals, layout, location | Salavakkam | Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube | Not RERA-registered: progress only, no booking or prices |
| NRI and Gulf investors | Trust, documents, remote updates | FOUR·94, Salavakkam | WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn | No FEMA/tax advice, no returns; Hindi and Arabic content is welcome |
| Landowners and partners | Joint development, founder credibility | Company | LinkedIn, founder's profile | Keep it about approach and process, not deal terms |

Buyers compare us with large developers. Aim to look as organised as they are without overclaiming.
