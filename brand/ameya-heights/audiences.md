# Ameya Heights: audiences

Primary goal for every audience: **book a site visit** (or a video walk-through from abroad).
Secondary: WhatsApp conversation, document-pack or brochure request.

| Audience | What they want | Project | Best channels | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Occupiers leasing space (clinics, offices, retail, showrooms, banks) | Location, frontage, floor sizes, parking, timeline | FOUR·94 | GBP, LinkedIn, Instagram | Lease only; areas are "about"; no rent figures |
| Chennai plot buyers | Clear title, approvals, layout, location | Salavakkam | Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube | Approvals to follow: progress only, no booking or prices |
| NRIs and OCIs (Gulf, US, UK, Singapore) | Documents, remote updates, a founder who answers | FOUR·94, Salavakkam | WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn | "Subject to applicable law"; no FEMA or tax advice; no returns |
| HNIs and family offices | Diligence, founder access | Company | LinkedIn, founder's profile | No yields, no co-investment terms in public |
| Landowners | Joint development, founder credibility | Company | LinkedIn, founder's profile | Process, not deal terms |
| Channel partners | A clear, honest partner process | FOUR·94 | LinkedIn, WhatsApp | Same rules as direct: no rates, no scarcity |

Buyers compare us with large developers. Aim to look as organised as they are without overclaiming,
and never name them.
