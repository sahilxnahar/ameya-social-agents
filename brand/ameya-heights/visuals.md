# Ameya Heights: visuals

Owner decision, 21 October 2026: social uses the Brand Canon system (13 July 2026).

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Architect Black | `#100F0D` | Backgrounds |
| Ink | `#16140F` | Secondary dark surfaces |
| Sand | `#ECE7DF` | Text on dark, light backgrounds |
| Stone | `#9A958C` | Captions, rules, metadata |
| Brass | `#C2A05B` | Marks and accents only |
| Deep brass | `#A07D34` | Brass text on light backgrounds |

- Brass is never a background fill. At most one brass accent or call to action per frame.
- No gradients. No other colours.

## Type

- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond
- Body and captions: Inter

## Logo

The interlaced knot mark with the wordmark. Keep clear space; never recolour outside this palette.

## Image rules

- Real site photos first, dated in the caption (month and year).
- Renders and AI keyframes carry "Artist's impression" on the image or in the first line of the caption.
- Concept boards are design direction, not the approved building. Do not post them as renders.
- Stock is labelled "illustrative". Never present stock aerials as Salavakkam. Jodhpur is stock only.
- Kill list: stock photography as hero, gradients, "LAUNCHING SOON" ribbons, collages.
- No people's faces without consent; blur number plates.
